Privacy policy

FocuLY for Android · Last updated October 31, 2026

FocuLY is an offline focus app published by Veil. The Android app does not request internet access, create an account, show ads, or use analytics. Your focus settings and history remain on your device unless you choose to export them.

Information used by the app

  • Rules, schedules, focus profiles, timers, overrides, preferences, and any artwork you choose are saved locally so the app can work.
  • If you grant Usage Access, FocuLY reads Android's app usage information to show local insights and apply rules based on use. FocuLY does not delete Android's system usage history.
  • If you enable Accessibility protection, FocuLY checks the foreground app, supported browser address fields, and selected short-video controls. On-screen text is checked in memory to apply your rules; it is not recorded or uploaded.
  • If you enable notifications, FocuLY can show session status and optional reminders. Optional device-owner controls on a dedicated device may manage app availability under the rules you choose.
  • If you set a device-controls PIN, FocuLY stores a salted, one-way PIN hash locally, not the PIN itself.

Sharing and retention

The Android app does not send your data to Veil or third parties. Data remains on your device until you change or delete it. You can export a JSON copy in Settings → Privacy & data. You can delete app data there when Strict mode is inactive, or clear the app's storage in Android settings. An exported file is managed by the destination you select. Uninstalling the app also removes its private local data, subject to Android's device management controls and any copies you exported.

Permissions and choices

Accessibility, Usage Access, and notifications are optional and can be withdrawn in Android settings. Without Accessibility protection, timers still work but app, site, and feed blocking may not. Device-owner controls require separate setup on a dedicated device and are not part of ordinary onboarding.

This website

The FocuLY website does not ask for an account or use first-party analytics. Its hosting provider may process ordinary server request data, such as an IP address and browser information, to deliver and protect the site. Following a download link takes you to GitHub, which has its own privacy practices.

Questions

For privacy questions or requests, contact Veil through the FocuLY GitHub issue tracker. Please do not post private information in a public issue.
